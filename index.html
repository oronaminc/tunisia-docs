Step 1 · Municipality

Request the documents at the civil status office

البلدية · قسم الحالة المدنية

The birth certificate is issued on the spot or within 24 hours. If the municipality does not issue a certificate of single status, ask at the counter which document replaces it.

Step 2 · Notary

Get the apostille

عدل إشهاد · الأبوستيل

Official fee10 dinars per document issued within the notary's district, 20 dinars outside it
TimeSame day
Destination countrySouth Korea (Republic of Korea)
The apostille is a certificate added to the document itself. Without it, the Korean district office may refuse the document.
Step 3 · To be confirmed

Police record (Bulletin n°3)

بطاقة عدد 3

This document may be requested for the residence permit. We will confirm whether it is needed. Official time: 8 days in Tunisia, 15 days outside Tunisia.

Send to Korea
