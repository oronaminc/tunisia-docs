Documents from Tunisia

These papers are for Bella: she needs them for her residence status (visa) and other administrative procedures in South Korea, and they can only be issued in Tunisia. We would be very grateful for your help. This page simply explains what they are, whenever it is convenient for you.

The documents

  • Birth certificate — extrait de naissance, مضمون ولادة (full copy, in French) · 2 copies
  • Certificate of single status — certificat de célibat, شهادة عزوبية (in French) · 2 copies

Both are issued by the municipality (البلدية · قسم الحالة المدنية), usually the same day.

The apostille

Korea asks for an apostille (الأبوستيل) on each document. In Tunisia, a notary (notaire, عدل إشهاد) adds it to the original.

WhereAny notary in Tunisia
Official fee10 to 20 dinars per document
TimeUsually the same day
Destination countrySouth Korea (Republic of Korea)
